# Manuscript Captions: Drought Diagnostics (V0)

**Figure 1. Relative Monthly Drought Area (SSM)**
*Caption:* Temporal evolution of the percentage of the domain area under moderate drought (D1, percentile ≤ 20%) or worse, based on Surface Soil Moisture (SSM). The time series compares the Open Loop (OPL) against the two SMAP assimilation experiments (DA-NoCDF and DA-CDF). Note that the percentiles are derived from a relative 2016–2020 reference distribution. The differences highlight the diagnostic sensitivity of the model-derived drought classification to the assimilated observations.

**Figure 2. Relative Monthly Drought Area (RZSM)**
*Caption:* Temporal evolution of the percentage of the domain area under moderate drought (D1, percentile ≤ 20%) or worse, based on Root-Zone Soil Moisture (RZSM). The time series compares the Open Loop (OPL) against the two SMAP assimilation experiments (DA-NoCDF and DA-CDF). 

**Figure 3. Spatial Frequency of Drought Conditions (SSM)**
*Caption:* Spatial distribution of the frequency of moderate drought conditions (D1, percentile ≤ 20%) based on SSM for the 2016–2020 period. The panels contrast the OPL frequency with the frequencies resulting from DA-NoCDF and DA-CDF. 

**Figure 4. Spatial Frequency of Drought Conditions (RZSM)**
*Caption:* Spatial distribution of the frequency of moderate drought conditions (D1, percentile ≤ 20%) based on RZSM for the 2016–2020 period. The panels contrast the OPL frequency with the frequencies resulting from DA-NoCDF and DA-CDF. 

**Figure 5. DA-Induced Drought Transitions (OPL to DA-NoCDF)**
*Caption:* Average monthly percentage of pixels experiencing a DA-induced shift in their discrete drought classification when moving from the OPL to the DA-NoCDF state. Transitions are categorized as unchanged, alleviated, intensified, introduced drought, or removed drought.

**Figure 6. DA-Induced Drought Transitions (OPL to DA-CDF)**
*Caption:* Average monthly percentage of pixels experiencing a DA-induced shift in their discrete drought classification when moving from the OPL to the DA-CDF state.

**Figure 7. Mean Drought Area Stratified by Land Cover**
*Caption:* Average percentage of area experiencing moderate drought (D1) stratified by dominant land cover types, highlighting the diagnostic sensitivity across different vegetation classes.
