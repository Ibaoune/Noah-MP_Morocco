# Manuscript Captions: Drought Diagnostics (V0)

**Figure 1. Relative Monthly Drought Area (SSM)**
*Caption:* Temporal evolution of the percentage of the domain area under moderate drought (D1, percentile ≤ 20%) or worse, based on Surface Soil Moisture (SSM). The time series compares the Open Loop (OPL) against the two SMAP assimilation experiments (DA-NoCDF and DA-CDF). Note that the percentiles are derived from an OPL-based pooled 2016–2020 reference distribution. This is a model-derived relative diagnostic and not a climatological drought index. D1 and D2 thresholds are emphasized, as extreme percentiles (D3/D4) are statistically unstable over the short 5-year reference window.

**Figure 3. Relative Drought-Class Frequency (SSM)**
*Caption:* Spatial distribution of the relative frequency of moderate drought classes (D1, percentile ≤ 20%) based on SSM for the 2016–2020 period. The panels contrast the OPL frequency with the frequencies resulting from DA-NoCDF and DA-CDF. The frequencies reflect DA-induced diagnostic sensitivity relative to the OPL-based 2016–2020 reference distribution.

**Figure 5. DA-Induced Drought Transitions (OPL to DA-NoCDF)**
*Caption:* Average monthly percentage of pixels experiencing a DA-induced shift in their discrete drought classification when moving from the OPL to the DA-NoCDF state. Transitions are categorized as unchanged, alleviated, intensified, introduced drought, or removed drought.

**Figure 6. DA-Induced Drought Transitions (OPL to DA-CDF)**
*Caption:* Average monthly percentage of pixels experiencing a DA-induced shift in their discrete drought classification when moving from the OPL to the DA-CDF state.

**Figure 7. Mean Drought Area Stratified by Land Cover**
*Caption:* Average percentage of area experiencing moderate drought (D1) stratified by dominant land cover types, highlighting the diagnostic sensitivity across different vegetation classes.
